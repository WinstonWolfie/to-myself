In Stoicism, impression is simply imprinting left by [[external]]s. The Greek term is *φαντασία*, from which English gets *fantasy*.

It's the way you perceive things, like you would think a face is good looking or not. Like externals, it can be acted upon by [[virtue]]. After the mind receives the impression, it can decide whether to give assent or withhold it. This is done by the [[command centre]]. People whose command entre is unhealthy make fewer judgements like this, or none at all.
